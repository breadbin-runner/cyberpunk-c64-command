# Support / Hilfe

If something does not work, please open a GitHub issue in this repository.
You may write in English or German. A useful report includes:

1. The app version and macOS version.
2. Your C64 Ultimate model and firmware version, if the issue involves the device.
3. The steps that led to the problem, what you expected and what happened.
4. The exact error message, if one appeared.

Please leave passwords, access tokens and private files out of reports and
screenshots. You can replace your local network address with an example such
as 192.168.x.x.

**Deutsch:** Probleme und Wünsche kannst du als GitHub-Issue melden. Nenne
App-Version, macOS-Version, bei Gerätefragen Modell und Firmware sowie die
Schritte bis zum Fehler. Passwörter und private Dateien bitte nicht mitsenden.

The app is a small independent project. Reports are welcome; response times
may vary.
